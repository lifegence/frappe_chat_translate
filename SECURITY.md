# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately by e-mail to **contact@lifegence.com**, not in a public
issue. Include the affected version, the steps to reproduce and the impact you observed.

We will acknowledge the report within 5 business days and agree on a disclosure date with you.

## Scope

This policy covers this app (`frappe_chat_translate`). Issues in ClefinCode Chat itself should be
reported to the ClefinCode maintainers; if they also affect this app's access checks, please tell us too.
